DALI Intel

See what changes on your competitors' public web pages.

Competitor web-page monitoring for any business, with UK public contract awards included.

Coming soon: DALI Intel checks the public web pages you choose, such as a competitor's pricing, services or news pages, and reports what changed since the last check. It filters out changes that are only reordered or re-wrapped text. Each report shows when a change was first seen and which pages couldn't be checked, with all times in UK time, and you can export it as HTML, CSV or Markdown. Pages a site's robots.txt disallows, pages that block automated access, and pages that only load with JavaScript are skipped and shown as 'not checked'.

02

Included extra

Included extra for businesses that bid for UK public-sector work: a weekly brief of contract awards from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder in the sectors and regions you tell us (matched on each notice's CPV and region codes), showing who won, the award value and number of bids where the notice publishes them, and what up to 10 competitors you name have won (matched by Companies House number, or by name, flagged as probable).
