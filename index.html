DALI Intel

See what changes on your competitors' public web pages.

Competitor web-page monitoring for any business.

DALI Intel checks the public web pages you choose, such as a competitor's pricing, services or news pages, and reports what changed since the last check. It filters out changes that are only reordered or re-wrapped text. Each report shows when a change was first seen and which pages couldn't be checked, with all times in UK time, and you can export it as HTML, CSV or Markdown. Pages a site's robots.txt disallows, pages that block automated access, and pages that only load with JavaScript are skipped and shown as 'not checked'.

Included with a paid subscription: a digest of the last 7 days of changes on the pages you watch, which you can open or download as HTML or Markdown.

Try DALI Intel in your browser: create an account, sign in and add the pages you want to watch without paying. Checking pages, reports and exports need a paid subscription.
